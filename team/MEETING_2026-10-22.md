## Facts to use if asked

- Original table: 18,980 rows and 39 columns. Keeping cells with at least 10% park overlap gives 43 cells, 15,695 rows and 566 positive targets.
- Full models use 23 weather, rainfall, terrain and recent-fire predictors. The conditions-only comparison uses 20, excluding recent fire. Target-day counts, labels, identifiers, coordinates and dates are excluded from the main predictors.
- Weekly and rotating blocked evaluations measure within-season interpolation and can use later training weeks. The forward test fits earlier dates and evaluates later ones. None establishes transfer to a different fire season.
- In the forward test, the full model missed all 32 positive rows with no detection on the feature day at its validation-selected threshold. This subset does not establish confirmed new ignition.
- The target is a satellite-detection proxy. A false positive against that label is not independently confirmed evidence that no fire was present.

| Forward test, full-model comparison | True positives | False positives | False negatives | AP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Persistence: repeat today's fire state | 13 | 36 | 32 | 0.081 |
| XGBoost | 13 | 35 | 32 | 0.186 |

AP measures ranking; alert counts depend on the chosen threshold. A better ranking score does not by itself establish a useful alert policy. These counts are cell-days, not independent incidents. Preserve the evaluated thresholds and test results; changes motivated by observed test outcomes are subsequent exploratory experiments.
