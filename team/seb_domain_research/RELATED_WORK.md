# Related-work starting synthesis for Seb's review

nonlinear feature relationships; it does not guarantee improved transfer on our
single-season dataset. The supplied Australian environmental-analytics paper
motivates multi-source bushfire research, but its task and headline metrics are
not directly comparable to our next-day cell/day AP evaluation. The group's
revised logistic comparison ranked better than XGBoost on final dates.
