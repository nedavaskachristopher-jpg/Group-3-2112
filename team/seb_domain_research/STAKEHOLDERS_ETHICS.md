# Stakeholders, ethics and recommendations for review

Frozen results give two concrete burdens: the higher cutoff misses 33 of 49
positive cell-days with 39 false alerts; the lower misses 15 but generates 2,434
false alerts. These counts do not equal independent incidents or staffing costs.
See [final evidence](../../research/reports/final_holdout.md).

| Potential stakeholder | Consequence suggested by the evidence | Proposed safeguard; consultation status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency services | Frequent false alerts could consume review attention; misses could create false reassurance | No operational use; define requirements with practitioners; no consultation recorded |
| NPWS/land managers | Coarse cells and imperfect burn exclusion limit management interpretation | Verify labels/geometry and require human review; source-owner confirmation pending |
| Residents/councils | A percentage may be mistaken for a reliable probability or assurance of safety | Label experimental scores clearly; do not publish as an official warning |
| Traditional Owners and affected communities | Future use could affect land-management decisions without appropriate context | Appropriate consultation/governance would be needed; none is claimed here |

Recommended research needs are independent seasons/events, observation/ignition
evidence, fuel data and forecast-time availability, followed by calibration and
decision-policy validation on new independent data. No financial loss, response
capacity or stakeholder agreement is invented. Seb should substantiate and review
the interpretation before the group adopts the recommendations.
